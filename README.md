# AI Reliance and Perceived Technology Dependence Among Secondary-School Students

A case study of NIS Karaganda, Kazakhstan, by **Azat Zarukul** (Global Perspectives and Project Work, May 2026).

The project examines how **159 surveyed students** describe their use of generative AI, independent study, verification of AI output, and perceived reliance. It combines questionnaire items and open-ended responses. The findings describe students at one school; they do not establish that AI caused changes in critical thinking or represent all Kazakhstani adolescents.

**Read the study:** [research.md](research.md) or [download the Word version](AI_Reliance_Research_Final.docx).

The source manuscript's first frequency chart was identified as erroneous and is excluded. The remaining Likert chart is retained with a clear note that its item-level response counts cannot be verified without the original form export. Raw student responses are not published.
