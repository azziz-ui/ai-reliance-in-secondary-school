# AI Reliance in Secondary School

**AI Reliance and Perceived Technology Dependence Among Secondary-School Students**

*A case study of NIS Karaganda, Kazakhstan · May 2026*

An exploratory study by **Azat Zarukul** for Global Perspectives and Project Work. It asks how students use generative AI for schoolwork, when they turn to it, and whether they check what it tells them.

[Read the full study](research.md) · [Download the Word manuscript](ai%20reliance.docx) · [Revision notes](REVIEW_NOTES.md)

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Participants** | 159 students at Nazarbayev Intellectual School, Karaganda |
| **Method** | Voluntary, anonymous survey with multiple-choice, Likert-scale, and open-ended questions |
| **Focus** | AI use, study habits, verification, perceived reliance, and independent thinking |
| **Scope** | One-school case study; not a national estimate or a causal test |

Students describe AI as useful for explanations, ideas, and saving time. Some also report relying on ready-made answers or putting less effort into independent work. These are **students’ self-reported experiences**, not evidence that AI caused a decline in critical thinking.

> **A note on the figures:** The source manuscript’s first AI-use frequency chart was found to be erroneous and is omitted. The remaining [Likert-response chart](research.md#42-habit-academic-pressure-and-verification) is shown for context, but its item-level response counts cannot be verified without the original survey export. Raw student responses are not published.
